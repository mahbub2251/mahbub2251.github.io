01About

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Information Systems at the University of Maryland, Baltimore County (UMBC), with my dissertation defense expected in December 2026. My research, conducted in the NSF-funded Latent Lab under Dr. James Foulds, centers on the evaluation of trustworthy and fair AI systems — work that I believe aligns closely with this position's focus on AI, meta-analysis, and evidentiary rigor.

A central thread of my research has been synthesizing a fragmented and often inconsistent fairness-metrics literature into coherent, generalizable evaluation frameworks.
