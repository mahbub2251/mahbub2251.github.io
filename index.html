01About

I'm a PhD candidate working at the intersection of cognitive science and computational modeling. My research asks how people form and update beliefs when evidence is noisy or incomplete, and what that reveals about the algorithms the brain uses to make decisions.

Before starting my PhD, I completed a master's degree in Experimental Psychology and spent two years as a research assistant studying perceptual learning. I care about open, reproducible science and spend part of my time building tools that make analysis pipelines easier to share.
