01About

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Information Systems at the University of Maryland, Baltimore County (UMBC), advised by Dr. James Foulds (Latent Lab), with Dr. Shimei Pan as co-advisor. My research focuses on the evaluation of trustworthy and fair AI systems.

A central thread of my research has been synthesizing a fragmented and often inconsistent fairness-metrics literature into coherent, generalizable evaluation frameworks.
